Purrwood

Explore a quiet world, invite cats in, and make a place they love. Purrwood is a cat-collecting game for iPhone and Android.

This site holds the pages the app links to. It collects nothing: no cookies, no analytics, no scripts.

On this site

  • Help and support: how accounts, codes, offline play and saves work, and how to reach us.
  • Privacy policy: what the app stores, where, and how to delete it.
  • Account emails: the page an account email points at. Nothing happens here; codes are entered in the app.
Purrwood is an independent game. The app is the only place your account is used.
