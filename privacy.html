Privacy policy

What Purrwood keeps, and why

Last updated 16 September 2026.

Purrwood is a game. It keeps the least it can to let you play and to bring your cats back on another phone. It shows no advertising, uses no analytics or tracking, and never sells or shares your information.

What is stored

DataWhenWhy
Your game save: cats, rooms, items, progress, in-game currencyAlways, on your phone; in your account when you have a connectionTo play, and to restore your garden on another phone
A random account identifierWhen you continue as a guest or create an accountTo tell your save from everyone else's
Email addressOnly if you create an email accountTo sign you in and to send sign-in and password codes
Password, stored as a one-way hash that cannot be read backOnly if you create an email accountTo sign you in
Apple account identifierOnly if you sign in with AppleTo sign you in. We receive Apple's identifier and, if you allow it, your email, which may be Apple's private relay address
Purchase receiptsOnly if you buy something in the shopTo deliver what you bought and to handle refunds. Payment details stay with Apple or Google and never reach us
Sound and preference settingsAlwaysKept on your phone only

Purrwood does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or advertising identifiers.

Where it is stored

Your save is kept on your phone in the app's private storage. Sign-in tokens are kept in the phone's secure keychain. When you have a connection, the save and account are stored with Supabase, the hosting service Purrwood's account system runs on, in a database only the game's own server code can write to. Supabase is a processor acting on Purrwood's instructions; see Supabase's privacy policy.

Sign-in and password emails are sent by that account system to the address you gave. Nothing else is emailed.

What Purrwood does not do

  • No advertising, no ad networks, no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, tracking or profiling, in the app or on this website.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of your data with anyone.
  • No reading of your password: it is hashed before it is stored, and the app never keeps it.
  • No cookies on this website. These pages are plain files.

Your choices

  • Play without an account. As a guest, nothing about you is stored beyond a random identifier and your save.
  • Delete everything. Settings → Delete account and save removes your account, your save and your sign-in details from our systems at once. Uninstalling the app removes the copy on your phone.
  • Take your save with you. Settings can export an encrypted backup file that only your passphrase opens.
  • Change or remove your email. Contact us using the details below, or delete the account and start again.

Children

Purrwood is suitable for all ages and asks for nothing about a player's age, name or identity. An email address is needed only for the optional account, and a parent or guardian may use their own. If you believe a child has created an account you did not agree to, contact us and it will be removed.

Retention

Your account and save are kept until you delete them. Accounts that were never used again are not mined or sold; they simply sit until deleted. Purchase receipts are kept as long as the stores require for refunds.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it and the app links here, so the current version is always this page.

Questions or requests: open an issue on the Purrwood project page. Do not include passwords, codes or backup passphrases.

Purrwood is an independent game. The app is the only place your account is used.
