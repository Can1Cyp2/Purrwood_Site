Help and support

How Purrwood works

Most questions are answered below. If yours is not, the contact details are at the end.

Accounts

Do I need an account?

No. Continue as guest starts a garden right away. An account is only what lets that garden come back on another phone or after a reinstall. You can add an email or Apple sign-in to a guest garden at any time from Settings, and the garden stays exactly as it is.

I created an account and got an email with a six-digit code. What do I do with it?

Type it into the app on the Enter your code screen. Codes are entered in the app only; there is no link to tap and nothing to do on this website. If the app has closed, open it again and the code screen is still waiting.

My code says it is wrong or expired.

Codes last a short while. Go back, ask for another, and enter the newest one. Check that you are entering it on the same phone the request came from.

I forgot my password.

On the sign-in screen choose I forgot my password. If the address has an account, a code is emailed to it; enter the code and choose a new password. Finishing a reset signs out every other phone that was using the account.

I did not get the email.

Check spam and any promotions folder, and confirm the address is spelled correctly. For safety the app shows the same message whether or not an address has an account, so a missing email is not proof that one exists.

Playing offline

Purrwood works without a connection. Exploring, collecting, feeding and furnishing all happen on your phone, and your progress is saved there first. When a connection returns, the save is sent to your account and the newest one is kept. The shop's real-money purchases, signing out and deleting an account need a connection.

Settings shows whether the garden is saved to your account, saving, or playing offline, with a button to try syncing now.

Saves and backups

I got a new phone. How do I get my cats back?

Sign in with the same email or Apple account and the garden comes down from your account. If you only ever played as a guest and never added an account, the save lives on the old phone only. You can also move a save by hand with an encrypted backup file from Settings, which needs a passphrase you choose.

Two phones show different progress.

Each phone plays on its own and syncs when it can. The account keeps whichever save has gone furthest. Playing on both at once is fine; playing on one while the other is offline for a long time means the further one wins when both reconnect.

Deleting your account

Settings → Delete account and save removes the account and everything stored under it. It needs a connection, and it cannot be undone. The copy on your phone is removed when the app is uninstalled.

Contact

Bugs, ideas and account trouble: open an issue on the Purrwood project page. Never post a password, code or backup passphrase there; nobody at Purrwood will ever ask for one.
