Account email

Enter the code in the app

Purrwood confirms sign-ups and password resets with a six-digit code typed into the app, not with a link. This page exists only so that an account email always has somewhere safe to point.

Open Purrwood on your phone and enter the code from the email on the Enter your code screen.

That link has expired or was already used. Open the app and ask for a new code.

Nothing is stored or sent by this page. If your browser shows anything after a # in the address, it has been discarded.

Did not ask for this?

Someone may have typed your address by mistake. Without the code, nothing can happen to your address or to any account, and you can ignore the email.
